# Keep

## Register

product

The whole surface is an app window: a workspace list, a tab strip, terminal
panes. There is no marketing page, no landing, nothing where the design is the
thing being sold. Design here serves someone already at work.

## Users & Purpose

One developer, and anyone who works the way they do: hands on the keyboard,
several projects open at once, a terminal filling the screen for hours.

Keep holds terminal workspaces that outlive the window. Closing the app loses
nothing; the shells keep running in a daemon and reopening finds them where
they were. The job it does is remembering, so the person does not have to.

The scene the interface is designed for: a dark room at night, one large
display, a terminal filling it, and this window's chrome as a thin margin
glanced at between keystrokes. That scene decides the theme. A light surface
here would be a lamp beside the content.

## Brand Personality

It has its own character rather than borrowing one. Not Zed, not Finder, not a
neutral shell.

The character it reaches for is a terminal that grew a memory: typographically
literal, dense without being cramped, honest about being made of text. Where it
decorates, it decorates with something that is also information.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS.** Cards everywhere, one accent blue, rounded pills, air used
  to signal quality. If a screen could belong to any dashboard, it is wrong.
- **Grey on grey.** Everything the same tone, no hierarchy, the active thing
  indistinguishable from the rest. This is the failure mode of the monochrome
  road and the one to watch for, because this app has been walking it.

## Strategic Principles

- **Colour carries information or it does not appear.** State and selection,
  nothing else. Decoration in colour is how this app would become the thing it
  is trying not to be.
- **Identifiers are set in the terminal's own typeface.** A workspace name is
  terminal content, not a UI label, and setting it in the face the terminal
  uses is the difference between chrome bolted on and chrome that belongs.
- **Chrome recedes, content does not.** Every control is quiet at rest and
  legible under the pointer.
- **The daemon owns the facts.** The interface is a view of them and never a
  second copy.

## Accessibility

Not yet stated by the owner. Assumed: contrast strong enough to find the
active row at a glance from a normal seating distance, and no meaning carried
by hue alone. Where a dot carries state, its shape carries it too.
